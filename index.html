All questions
What requirements do you have to join?

We welcome any mastery rank. We just ask that you log in at least once every 14 days and are comfortable speaking English.

How can I join?

Join our Discord, head to the tickets channel, and open a "Member/Recruitment Application" ticket.

I'm new, is this still a good clan for me?

Yes, any MR is welcome. We try to be as friendly and helpful to newer players as possible.

I'm not 18+, can I still join?

Of course. We have a zero-tolerance policy on sexually explicit material, so the clan is suitable for anyone old enough to play the game.
