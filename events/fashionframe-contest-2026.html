The contest

We're running a Captura and fashion contest right now, and 8,000 platinum is up for grabs.

First place wins 5,000 plat, second wins 2,000 and third wins 1,000. Show us a Fashionframe you're proud of, with a Captura screenshot and the gear behind the look, and post it in our Discord before the deadline.

  3. 3rd place1,000 plat
  4. Runner up5 XP*

Contest rules

Rule following is one of the judging criteria, so check your post against this list before you submit.

  • No external edited screenshots. Minor edits like cropping are fine.
  • Include a screenshot of the frame in your orbiter or backroom.
  • Include images of the attachments, skins and anything else you used.
  • One submission per person.
  • Put your IGN and platform in the post.

How to enter

  1. Join our Discord if you haven't already.
  2. Find the contest post and read it through.
  3. Reply to it with your screenshots, your IGN and your platform.

How entries are judged

Every fashionframe is scored on six things.

Cohesion and looks

How well the pieces of the build go together, and how it looks overall.

Originality

How much of the idea is your own.

Attachments used

Scored as 0, 1, or 2 or more.

Captura quality

How good the screenshot itself is.

Effort

How much work went into the look and the shot.

Rule following

Whether your post meets the rules above.

Contest questions

When is the deadline?

Submissions close on Saturday, November 21, 2026 at 22:59 UTC. If your browser allows it, this time shows in your own time zone.

Where do I post my entry?

In the replies under the contest post in our Discord.

What do I need to include?

A screenshot of the frame in your orbiter or backroom, images of the attachments and skins you used, and your IGN and platform.

Can I enter more than once?

No. Each person gets one submission.

Can I edit my screenshots?

Only for minor things like cropping. Screenshots edited with outside tools aren't allowed.

How much can I win?

5,000 plat for first place, 2,000 for second and 1,000 for third.
