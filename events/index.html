General Giveaways

We believe in sharing the spoils of the Origin System. BMME hosts regular giveaways to give back to our community, help newer players build out their inventories, and reward long-time members.

Giveaways are held on our Discord and are completely free to enter for all clan members. There are no requirements or pay-to-win mechanics.

What giveaways do we run?

We mostly do giveaways of skins and collections, and sometimes also give away plat.

How often do we host them?

We try hosting giveaways whenever there’s a new collection or skin out that people might want, with smaller giveaways in-between.

Who is eligible to participate?

Anyone can participate, as long as they’re in the Discord, and in our clan!

How do I enter giveaways?

Giveaways run exclusively in our Discord #giveaways channel. Simply click the reaction button to enter one.

How are winners selected?

Winners are automatically selected by Giveaway Boat, so that everything remains fair.

Can members sponsor giveaways?

Yes! Members are always welcome to create their own giveaways. If you’d like to create one yourself, simply make a Giveaway/Event Application ticket.
