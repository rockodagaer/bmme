How to join

  1. Join our Discord.
  2. Go to the tickets channel, open a "Member/Recruitment Application" ticket, and ask for an invite.
  3. Accept the clan invite in game.

That's it. Any mastery rank is welcome, even if you just finished Vor's Prize.

What we ask

Log in at least once every 30 days and be comfortable speaking English (the language page explains why). Everything else is in the rules.

New to clans?

Clan dojos have research labs, and a lot of weapons and gear can only be built from clan research. Being in a clan also gets you into clan and alliance chat, where people ask questions and look for squads. Our events and giveaways get announced in the Discord.

Other clans in Lotus' Gardens

If we're not the right fit, Lost Entities and Rabbit Masquerade are the other two Mountain clans in our alliance. Their Discord links are on the alliance page.
