Clan

What requirements do you have to join?

We just ask for a mastery rank of 7 or higher, logging in at least once every 14 days, and being comfortable speaking English.

How can I join?

Join our Discord, head to the tickets channel, and open a "Member/Recruitment Application" ticket.

I'm not 18+, can I still join?

Of course. We have a zero-tolerance policy on sexually explicit material, so the clan is suitable for anyone old enough to play the game.

Why do I have to join the Discord within 48 hours?

We like keeping the community close-knit, and having everyone in one shared space is the easiest way to make that happen.

What happens if I don't join in time?

You may be removed from the clan, so it's best to hop in as soon as you can.

What's the clan's playstyle?

We welcome any playstyle and skill level. We can help newer players get through the star chart, or help endgame players squeeze out the most resources.

I'm new, is this still a good clan for me?

If you're MR7 or above, yes. We try to be as friendly and helpful to newer players as possible.

Do I need to be active to join giveaways or events?

Nope. There's no activity threshold to participate — anyone can join in unless stated otherwise. Check out our Events & Giveaways page to see what we run!

Do you use voice chat?

Yes, we have active voice channels that get used often when people play together.

I'm already a member, can I invite a friend?

Of course. Just open a "Member/Recruitment Application" ticket the same way you did.

What are the basic rules I have to follow?

Be kind, and no hateful behavior of any kind.

Alliance

What is Lotus' Gardens, exactly?

Lotus' Gardens is an in-game alliance meant to encourage socializing and resource-sharing, and to give members of every allied clan a more welcoming experience. Learn more about our member clans on the Lotus' Gardens Alliance page.

If I join an allied clan, am I part of the alliance?

Yes — joining any of the four allied clans automatically makes you part of Lotus' Gardens. You can explore all member clans on our alliance page.

Can I switch between allied clans if I want something different?

We have a strict no-poaching policy: if you leave one allied clan, you can't join another allied clan for 30 days.
