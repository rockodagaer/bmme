Clan Transference

Glory in Death has been inactive for over 45 days, which meets the inactivity threshold set out in our Alliance rules (Rule 14.3). Under Rule 14.4, once a clan has been inactive that long, its members are free to transfer to other clans in the Alliance if they wish. This means the usual anti-poaching protections (Rule 14) don’t apply to you: you can move without any penalty or risk of a ban, and the clan you join won’t be punished for taking you in. Nobody is being kicked out or punished here. We just don’t want you stuck in a clan with no activity when there are active communities in the Alliance that would be glad to have you.

Below you can pick which Alliance clan you’d like to join: Big Muscly Men Enjoyers, Lost Entities, or Rabbit Masquerade. Take your time, look at what each clan offers, and choose the one that fits you best. If you have questions about the process, want to know more about a clan, or run into any trouble, reach out to Alliance leadership and we’ll help you get settled in. We’re glad to have you as part of Lotus’ Gardens and hope to see you in your new home soon!
