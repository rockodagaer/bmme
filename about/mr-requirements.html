Why do I need to be MR7+?

We put our Mastery Rank requirement to 7 or higher because we feel as if this creates the best general experience for everyone involved. This also makes it so that everybody will at least mildly know what they’re talking about.

How do I check my MR?

In Warframe, open your menu, hover over your profile picture, go to your profile, and it’ll be the first thing you see.

I’m low MR, but I’m really good. Can I still join?

NO ):<

I have a friend who isn’t MR7+ yet, can they join too?

This varies a lot from case to case, but we are willing to make exceptions for certain people.

I’m not MR7+ yet, can I still be in the Discord?

I mean… fine by me, we won’t check anyways.
