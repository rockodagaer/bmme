Why English-only?

We only accept people who speak English, because we want to be able to enforce proper moderation, and have 100% of our community be able to interact with each other.

Well, if you can read this, you definitely pass the threshold.

Do I have to be from the UK or America to join?

No. Anybody from anywhere around the world is allowed to join!
