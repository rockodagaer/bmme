Rules

Guidelines for all members of BMME and Lotus' Gardens.

← Back to BMME · Read the FAQ → · Lotus' Gardens Alliance →

No racism, homophobia, sexism, or hateful behavior in general.

This includes being overly passive aggressive, or intentionally making other members uncomfortable.

Avoid bringing up controversial topics or heavy drama in the in-game Clan Chat or Alliance Chat.

No spam or self-promotion (server invites, advertisements, etc.) without permission from a staff member.

This includes DMing fellow members.

No age-restricted or obscene content. This includes text, images, or links featuring nudity, sex, hard violence, or other graphically disturbing content.

Screensharing sexual or graphically disturbing content is also strictly disallowed.

Do not intentionally spoil content later in the game without warning, or labeling it properly as being a spoiler.

Make sure to use a spoiler tag in the Discord, and to not spoil in the Clan Chat or Alliance Chat!

You must be active at least once every 14 days on Warframe, otherwise you will be kicked.

Don’t worry, you can always open a ticket in the Discord, and you’ll usually be added back right away!

Every member has to be in the Discord.

We give a 48 hour grace period before kicking you.

To prevent getting kicked, make sure to respond to moderator PMs in-game!
