What is a mountain clan?

A mountain clan is a specific clan tier in Warframe that means that the clan has a max capacity of 300 members, with a 30x resource cost multiplier.

What other clan tiers are there?

The clan tiers go as follows: Ghost, Shadow, Storm, Mountain, and Moon.

Why are you a mountain clan?

We outgrew our old Storm clan tier, so we upgraded to Mountain to make room for more members. Technically we could increase our clan tier again at any time, but we currently do not see the need.

Why are these pills even clickable?

Because I wanted to make the “Events & Giveaways” pill clickable… but I forgot that I had to actually convey to the user that that’s even a thing you can do… SO CLICK ON THAT PILL, IT’S THE ONLY USEFUL ONE! ]:<
