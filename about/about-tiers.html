What is a storm clan?

A storm clan is a specific clan tier in Warframe that means that the clan has a max capacity of 100 members, with a 10x resource cost multiplier.

What other clan tiers are there?

The clan tiers go as follows: Ghost, Shadow, Storm, Mountain, and Moon.

Why are you a storm clan?

Technically we could increase our clan tier at any time, but we currently do not see the need.
